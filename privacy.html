StopSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

StopSnap is published by H&E TRADE LLC ("we").

Short version: StopSnap does not store or sell any personal data. Package labels are read only on your iPhone. With StopSnap Pro, and only if you allow it, route screenshots are read by AI and then discarded.

What the app processes

  • Route screenshots you choose to import. The app reads the stop numbers and addresses in them.
  • The camera view while you scan labels. Text on the label is read on your iPhone to find the matching stop.

What happens to that information

  • Package labels are read only on your device. The camera picture never leaves your iPhone.
  • Route screenshots, free version or AI reading off: read only on your device, using Apple's built-in text recognition.
  • Route screenshots, StopSnap Pro with AI reading allowed: the app asks before the first time. If you allow it, the screenshots you choose are sent over an encrypted connection to the StopSnap server, which passes them to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), to read the stops and returns the result to your iPhone. Our server does not save or log the screenshots. Anthropic does not use them to train its models and deletes them under its data retention policy. The screenshots are used for nothing else. You can turn AI reading off at any time in the app's 🌐 menu.
  • The route is kept only in the app's memory while it is open. It is not written to storage.
  • It is erased when you tap "End route and erase" or when the app is closed.
  • The screenshots stay in your Photos library, which you control. You can delete them at any time.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale of data, and no sharing except with the AI provider described above, only to read your route.

Subscriptions

StopSnap Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app checks Apple's signed transaction on your iPhone to know whether Pro is active. When AI reading is used, that signed transaction (which contains a transaction number, the plan and its dates, not your name) is sent with the screenshots so the server can confirm Pro and count the monthly reading limit. You can manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only for reading labels while the scan screen is open. Photos access is limited to the screenshots you pick in the system picker.

Children

StopSnap is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
