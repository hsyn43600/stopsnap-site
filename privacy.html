StopSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

StopSnap is published by H&E TRADE LLC ("we").

Short version: StopSnap does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app processes

  • Route screenshots you choose to import. The app reads the text in them (stop numbers and addresses) on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition.
  • The camera view while you scan labels. Text on the label is read on your iPhone to find the matching stop.

What happens to that information

  • It is processed only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • It is kept only in the app's memory while your route is open. It is not written to storage.
  • It is erased when you tap "End route and erase" or when the app is closed.
  • The screenshots stay in your Photos library, which you control. You can delete them at any time.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of data.

Subscriptions

StopSnap Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks Apple's signed receipt on your iPhone to know whether Pro is active. You can manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only for reading labels while the scan screen is open. Photos access is limited to the screenshots you pick in the system picker.

Children

StopSnap is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
