StopSnap Support

StopSnap shows the stop number for each package label, on your iPhone, offline.

Getting started

  1. Open your route's stop list and take screenshots from the top down to the last stop.
  2. In StopSnap, tap Import route screenshots and select them (any order).
  3. Tap Scan packages and point the camera at each label.
